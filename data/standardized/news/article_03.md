## Vietnam is one of those places where you can plan your whole trip around delicious eats. Not only are the country’s many dishes a treat for your palate, eating local food is one of the best ways to get to know Vietnamese culture and people. Dedicated foodies who go beyond famous eats like phởbánh mì
* * *
## North
### Subtle sophistication
There’s a Vietnamese saying that translates to ‘eat Northern, dress Southern.’ Locals in the capital city of [Hanoi](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/northern-vietnam/ha-noi) have a knack for complex recipes that bring out flavours from farm-fresh ingredients. For example, a bowl of steaming bún thang
### Long traditions
Many Northern Vietnamese recipes date back hundreds of years. The food here is heavily influenced by age-old traditions and practices. Bánh chưngbánh chưng
### Street food heaven
Food from the North tends to have mild flavours and highlights the natural taste of ingredients, and served with lots of herbs and condiments. Don’t be intimidated by the options that come with your phở
TRY IT:
Bún thang Thuận Lý, 33 Hàng Hòm, Hoàn Kiếm District, Hanoi
Phở bò Khôi Hói, 50 Hàng Vải, Hoàn Kiếm District, Hanoi
* * *
## Central
### Sauces and seafood
If you’re a fan of spices, sauces, and dips, you’ll love the food of Central Vietnam. Any local here will tell you that the secret to amazing food lies in the dipping sauce. Sauces in [Đà Nẵng](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/central-vietnam/da-nang), [Hội An](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/central-vietnam/hoi-an), and [Nha Trang](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/central-vietnam/nha-trang) go way beyond your average nước mắm[Huế](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/central-vietnam/hue).
### Huế cuisine
Huế is famous for its imperial legacy as well as its spicy food. No trip to Huế is complete without sitting on a sidewalk, slurping up every last drop of the rich, red spicy broth of a bún bò Huế
### Secret ingredients
Regarded as some of the most creative in the country, cooks in Central Vietnam have unique ways of flavouring food. In a perfect bowl of mì Quảngmì Quảng
TRY IT:
O Lé Dumplings, 104/17/9 Kim Long, Huế
Mì Quảng Bà Vị, 166 Lê Đình Dương, Hải Châu District, Đà Nẵng
* * *
## South
### Humble beginnings
Just like the people here, Southern Vietnamese cuisine is open to many influences. Locals in [Hồ Chí Minh City](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/southern-vietnam/ho-chi-minh) bring their own cooking styles from many different parts of Vietnam, creating a culinary hub in the city. Southern dishes are often humble and casual. Unlike some lunch-only eats in the North, most food stalls in Southern Vietnam open all day. The popular cơm tấmcơm tấm
* * *
* * *
### The sweet South
Vietnamese in the South are big on sweetness. A pinch of sugar or a dash of coconut milk makes everything taste better here. Cá kho tộcá kho tộ[Mekong Delta](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/southern-vietnam/mekong-delta). It has sweetness, bold flavours, and features freshwater fish from the Mekong River.
### Dining in the Delta
The verdant Mekong Delta region is all about green vegetables, freshwater fish, and abundant tropical fruits. You’re sure to eat well here. The nutrient-rich soil is so fertile that when cultivated by hardworking Vietnamese farmers, it yields beautiful produce and some of the sweetest fruits in the country. After a day of paddling along small canals surrounded by mangrove trees, a plate of freshly picked dragon fruit, mangosteen, or longan with honey tea is a signature Mekong Delta snack. Dining in the South is a very laid-back experience. Recipes are simple and people are welcoming. Vegetable hotpot on a straw mat in the backyard is a great way to get to know the locals. You’ll find they are just as sweet as the fruit they grow.
* * *
